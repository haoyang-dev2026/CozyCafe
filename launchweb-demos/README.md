# LaunchWeb concept demos

Open `index.html` to view the portfolio and its three sample sites:

- `atelier.html` — salon services and appointment request concept
- `crumb.html` — bakery menu and interactive sample basket
- `ruang.html` — movement studio with an interactive weekly class schedule

These are fictional concept demos. All business names, services, schedules and prices are sample content; there are no real appointment submissions, orders or payments. Replace placeholder details with facts supplied and approved by a real business before client launch.

The pages use plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Their photos and Google Fonts are loaded from external services, so the visuals need an internet connection.
